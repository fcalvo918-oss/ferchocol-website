Fabellume by Ferchocol Software

Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 13, 2026. This notice explains how the Fabellume Windows desktop application handles information, including the Microsoft Store edition. It separately explains how Ferchocol Software handles information submitted through the former beta program.

Scope of this notice

This notice covers the Fabellume Windows desktop application, including the Microsoft Store edition, developed by Ferchocol Software. The app privacy sections below apply to ordinary use of Fabellume. The later section titled “Former beta program” applies only to information that people submitted through the former beta signup process.

Media and project information

Fabellume accesses the photos, videos, audio, and project files you choose to open. It processes that media locally on your Windows computer to create previews and exported videos.

Saved projects can contain media filenames, file paths, metadata, title and subtitle text, timeline arrangements, and editing settings.

Local storage and temporary files

You choose where to save projects and exported videos. Fabellume also creates autosaves, recovery files, temporary previews, audio caches, and diagnostic logs in Windows application-data and temporary folders.

Deleting a project does not necessarily remove its associated recovery files, caches, previews, or logs. Those files may remain until you remove them separately or Windows cleans them up.

Diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logs may include filenames, folder paths, output locations, operating-system information, FFmpeg details, and technical errors. File paths can reveal a Windows username or personal filenames. Please review logs before voluntarily sharing them with Ferchocol Software for support.

Automatic uploads, advertising, and tracking

The Microsoft Store edition does not automatically upload your media, projects, or diagnostic logs to Ferchocol Software. It contains no advertising, behavioral tracking, or developer-operated analytics service.

Microsoft Store and external services

Microsoft handles Microsoft Store purchases and distribution under Microsoft’s own privacy policies. The Microsoft Store edition does not require a separate Fabellume account or product key.

If you save files in a cloud-synchronized or network folder, your storage provider may transfer those files according to your settings and that provider’s privacy practices. External website links open in your web browser.

Your controls and retention

You choose which files to import, where to save projects and exports, and what information to share. You can access and delete local files through Windows.

Local project files, exports, autosaves, recovery files, previews, caches, and logs remain on your computer according to their location and Windows cleanup behavior. As noted above, deleting a project does not necessarily delete every related file.

Security of local files

Local Fabellume files rely on your Windows account permissions and device-security settings. You are responsible for controlling access to your Windows account, storage locations, exported videos, and any diagnostic information you choose to share. No method of storing information can provide absolute security.

Information voluntarily sent for support

If you contact Ferchocol Software for support, we use the information you voluntarily provide to investigate and respond to your request. Review diagnostic logs and attachments before sending them because they may contain filenames, folder paths, a Windows username, or other personal information.

Former beta program

Separate from Store app use: Fabellume beta testing has concluded, and the public beta signup form has been removed from the Ferchocol Software website. Applications are closed. The disclosures in this section apply only to information previously submitted through the former beta signup process. Ordinary use of the Microsoft Store edition does not submit this information to Ferchocol Software.

During the beta signup process, applicants submitted their name, email address, Windows version, computer type, video-editing experience, intended project, and agreement to provide feedback.

Applicants were instructed not to submit passwords, financial details, government identification numbers, health information, private media, or other sensitive personal information through the beta application.

How beta applicant information was used

Ferchocol Software used beta applicant information to:

  • review and evaluate beta applications;
  • select a balanced group of testers with suitable Windows systems and experience levels;
  • contact applicants about selection, access, instructions, updates, surveys, and beta participation;
  • understand likely projects and testing needs; and
  • administer, support, protect, and improve the Fabellume beta.

Ferchocol Software did not sell beta applicant information. Submitting an application did not guarantee acceptance or access.

Formspree and other disclosures

Beta applications were submitted through Formspree, which processed form information on behalf of Ferchocol Software and provided spam-prevention features. To the extent related records remain with Ferchocol Software or Formspree, Formspree may continue to store or otherwise process those records on Ferchocol Software’s behalf. Formspree’s handling of information is also governed by its own privacy terms.

Ferchocol Software may also disclose retained information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Beta applicant retention and choices

Ferchocol Software retains former beta applicant information only as long as reasonably necessary to maintain relevant records and meet legal or security obligations. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or anonymized when reasonably practicable. This notice does not state that all former applicant records have been deleted.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your former beta applicant information by emailing privacy@ferchocol.com. Some information may be retained where required for legal, fraud-prevention, or security purposes.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when Fabellume or its information practices change. The “Last updated” date identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@ferchocol.com. For general Fabellume support, email hello@ferchocol.com.

Important: This Fabellume notice supplements the general Ferchocol Software website privacy policy.
