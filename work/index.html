Vardex Construction Calculator

Focused Android software · $4.99 one-time purchase

Audience and problem

Vardex is designed for people who need practical starting estimates for common construction materials and project costs.

Constraints and solution

The application keeps the core workflow available offline and avoids accounts, advertisements, analytics, and tracking.

Important workflows

  • Concrete, Gravel, Paint, Roofing, Drywall, and Block Wall calculators
  • Combined Flooring, Tile & Carpet calculator
  • Construction unit converter
  • Saved and reusable job estimates
  • Adjustable material prices, labor rates, and markup
  • Professional PDF estimate creation and sharing
  • Light and dark themes
  • United States customary construction units

Current availability: Available for Android on Google Play for a one-time $4.99 purchase. Estimates are planning tools and do not replace professional measurements, supplier specifications, or contractor verification.

Fabellume

Windows desktop video editor · Limited beta applications may be open

Audience and intended solution

Fabellume is intended to help people create personal, story-driven videos from photos, video, audio, titles, transitions, effects, and animation.

Current status

Fabellume has not been publicly released. There is no public installer or public download, and applying for limited beta access does not guarantee acceptance.

Privacy and workflow considerations

Imported media is intended to be processed locally and is not intentionally uploaded to Ferchocol servers. No account, advertising, behavioral tracking, or intentional analytics or telemetry is currently planned for the beta.
