PROJECT FIT

Website work for practical small-business needs.

Who the service is for

Contractors, service businesses, local companies, and other small organizations that need a clear professional website or a focused update to an existing presence.

Typical projects

Informational business websites, service pages, portfolio presentation, contact and inquiry paths, and straightforward content organization.

What may be included

Mobile-responsive implementation, organized approved content, accessibility-minded details, essential on-page SEO, and launch or handoff steps agreed within the project scope.

What is not automatically included

E-commerce, payment processing, databases, user accounts, custom booking systems, paid software, premium assets, hosting fees, or ongoing maintenance unless specifically reviewed and agreed.
