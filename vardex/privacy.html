Vardex Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026
Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Vardex is a construction estimating and calculation application provided by Ferchocol Software. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Vardex.

Information You Enter

Vardex allows you to enter and save information used to create construction calculations and estimates. This may include:

  • Job and project names
  • Construction measurements and quantities
  • Material prices
  • Labor rates and labor hours
  • Markup percentages
  • Company or business name
  • Contact name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Website
  • Contractor or license information

Local Data Storage

Vardex stores user-entered information locally on your device so that your settings, material prices, and saved estimates can be available when you reopen the application.

Vardex does not currently require a user account or cloud account to use these features.

Data Collection and Transmission

Vardex does not currently collect or transmit user-entered job, estimate, company, or contact information to Ferchocol Software servers.

Vardex does not currently use its own analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services to collect information about your use of the application.

Ferchocol Software does not sell user-entered information to third parties.

PDF Estimates and Sharing

Vardex can generate PDF estimates using information you enter into the application.

PDF files are generated for your use. If you choose to save, copy, email, upload, print, or otherwise share an exported PDF, the handling of that file after it leaves Vardex may be subject to the privacy practices of the service, application, or recipient you choose.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by Vardex remains on your device until it is deleted through the application, removed as part of application data management, or removed when the application is uninstalled, depending on the operating system and device configuration.

Before uninstalling Vardex, users should save or export any information they wish to retain.

Internet and Third-Party Services

The current version of Vardex does not require Ferchocol Software-operated online services to perform its core construction calculation and estimating functions.

Your operating system, application store, or other services on your device may independently perform functions such as application installation, updates, security verification, backups, or other platform services. Those services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Vardex is intended as a construction estimating and productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children. Ferchocol Software does not knowingly use Vardex to collect personal information from children.

Security

Vardex is designed to keep its application data locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for their device and for files they export or share from Vardex.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vardex develops. For example, if a future version introduces cloud synchronization, user accounts, analytics, or other online services, this policy may be revised to describe those features and how information is handled.

The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
